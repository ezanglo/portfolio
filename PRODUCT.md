# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Freelance clients and companies evaluating whether to hire Ezra Anglo for React Native / mobile
product work — not recruiters screening for a full-time hire. Their situation: they need a mobile
product built, extended, maintained, or shipped to the App Store / Google Play, and are deciding
whether Ezra is the person who can own that end to end. Their job on the site: assess his
credibility and fit, then get in touch about a project.

## Product Purpose

A conversion-focused personal portfolio for Ezra Anglo, positioned as a Senior React Native &
Full-Stack Developer. It exists to attract freelance/contract engagements and company hires for
mobile product work, and succeeds when a qualified visitor is convinced to make contact about a
project — not when it reads as a job-seeking résumé.

## Positioning

An experienced full-stack engineer who specializes in building production mobile applications
with React Native and Expo — not a generic full-stack developer, not a web developer who also
does mobile, not an AI developer who happens to build apps, and not a React Native-only UI
developer. The differentiator a neighboring RN-only developer could not truthfully copy: 10+
years of full-stack engineering (APIs, auth, databases, infrastructure, AI-powered features) means
he can own a mobile product beyond the interface — a single person taking it from idea through
production and app-store launch, not a hand-off between three specialists.

## Operating Context

- Content is static TypeScript in `content/` — no CMS, no database. The only editor is the
  developer, working through Claude Code; changes ship by editing, committing, and deploying.
- The site previously ran on Payload CMS backed by Neon Postgres with uploads in Vercel Blob;
  that stack was removed in favor of static content and is not coming back.
- Beyond the homepage, dedicated SEO-targeted landing pages exist for specific offers:
  `/react-native-developer` and `/react-native-maintenance`, plus `/work` (case studies and
  project list) and `/insights` (articles).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- `content/` is the single source of truth for all copy, projects, case studies, experience, and
  skills. Project facts (descriptions, tags, links) are fixed; only classification fields may be
  derived from them.
- Never fabricate testimonials, client names, metrics, or outcomes. Where no verified evidence
  exists, the gap ships as an honest absence, not an invented claim.
- No full-time-job-seeking framing anywhere on the site (no "open to full-time roles" language or
  equivalent).

## Brand Commitments

- Name: "Ezra Anglo" / "Ezra Anglo Portfolio."
- Dark-locked theme — no light mode.
- One amber brand accent color; Space Grotesk (display) + Inter (body); pill-shaped buttons;
  16–20px card radii.
- Primary CTA framing: "Let's Build Your App" (client-acquisition), never job-seeking copy.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real case studies (Resiboo, OPIC, Finn AI, Stratos Command) written from the actual sibling
  codebases, not marketing copy.
- CV (`/cv-ezra-anglo.pdf`) and portrait image on hand.
- Services offered: React Native app development, mobile MVP development, taking over/maintaining
  existing React Native apps, full-stack mobile development, AI features for mobile products.
- No testimonials or client-confirmed metrics exist yet — future work must not invent them.

## Product Principles

1. Position as a full-stack engineer who specializes in production React Native/Expo mobile work —
   never RN-only, never generic full-stack, never AI-first.
2. Every claim must trace to real, verifiable evidence (code, docs, actual project work); no
   fabricated testimonials, metrics, or outcomes.
3. `content/` is the single, static source of truth — no CMS, no database.
4. This is a freelance/client-acquisition instrument, not a job-seeking résumé — avoid
   open-to-work framing.
5. Ownership is the differentiator: one person taking a mobile product from idea through
   app-store launch, including backend, infrastructure, and AI features — not a hand-off between
   specialists.
